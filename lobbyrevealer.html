TERMS OF SERVICE

In order to ensure every license is bound to a unique player, some information about your computer is stored by the licensing system. Hardware information about your PC is stored and encrypted in transit for security purposes, to ensure that two people cannot use the same license or slot simultaneously.

In some cases, such as this slotted version, your Discord ID is stored. This is done only with the full cooperation and consent of both the user and the seller. Your Discord ID is used solely to deliver lobby information and match results to your DMs, and for nothing else.
