Wintrade detector

Pull a player's recent matches, then see who they queue with, who they keep meeting on the other team, and how those games ended.

Enter a Riot ID and your API key to start.

Queues with

Everyone who was on the player's team in 2+ games, including duos and stacks. "In party" counts the games Riot listed them in the same party.

Players seen across games

Sorted by suspicion. In a random 10-player lobby, meeting the same non-party player again and again is unusual, and win/loss results that flip every time you meet are the classic trade pattern. Facing the same player on separate days is marked as a wintrade, and players under level 100 get a higher score. A K/D below 0.85 across their games against the target is flagged as poor.

This is a heuristic, not proof. Small ranks, off-peak hours and party queues also produce repeat matchups, so check the games yourself before accusing anyone.
